How SMS opt-in works

Endris Arroyo Negron (doing business as A/C Repair)
Registered messaging brand: Endris Arroyo Negron
Customer-care / missed-call SMS program — public description for messaging compliance review

Opt-in is not a paper form

There is no paper or printed consent form for this program. Opt-in is call-initiated / verbal-path customer care: the customer starts contact by calling the published business phone number. This page is the public description of that opt-in flow for reviewers.

Opt-in path (call-initiated customer care)

  1. The customer calls the published business phone number operated by Endris Arroyo Negron (A/C Repair) — our Twilio long code.
  2. If we miss the call, Endris Arroyo Negron may send the customer an automated SMS about that missed call and invite a reply.
  3. If the customer replies by text, Endris Arroyo Negron may continue the SMS conversation for customer care (understanding the request / arranging follow-up).
  4. The business owner may receive a notification SMS about the missed call.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

What this program is / is not

  • Is: first-party customer care from Endris Arroyo Negron (A/C Repair) after the customer initiates contact by calling.
  • Is not: a paper-form signup, affiliate marketing, third-party lead generation, purchased lists, or sharing opt-in data with other brands for their marketing.

Sample messages

  • “Endris Arroyo Negron: We missed your call. Reply here and we'll help with your A/C request. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.”
  • “Endris Arroyo Negron: Thanks — we got your reply and will follow up shortly. Msg frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out.”
  • “Endris Arroyo Negron: You're opted out and will no longer receive texts from this number. Reply HELP for help.”
