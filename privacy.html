Privacy Policy

A/C Repair (“we,” “us,” or “our”)
Effective date: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you call or text our business phone number, or use our missed-call follow-up service (LeadRescue / Project Relay).

Information we collect

  • Phone numbers (your caller ID when you call us, and numbers you text from)
  • Call-related metadata (time of call, missed/answered status)
  • SMS message content you send to us and automated replies we send
  • Business settings the account owner configures (business name, forwarding number, message templates)

How we use information

  • To return missed calls and respond to customer inquiries
  • To send automated SMS when a call to our business number is missed
  • To continue an SMS conversation you start by replying
  • To notify the business owner of missed calls and new leads
  • To operate, secure, and improve our service

SMS / text messaging

If you call our published business phone number and we miss the call, we may send you an SMS about that missed call and invite you to reply. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of further texts from us. Reply HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase.

We do not sell your phone number or SMS content. We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing.

Service providers

We use vendors to operate the service, including telephony/SMS providers (such as Twilio) and hosting/database providers. They process information only to provide services to us.

Data retention

We keep call/SMS lead records as long as needed to serve customers and operate the business, unless you request deletion where applicable law requires otherwise.

Your choices

  • Text STOP to opt out of SMS
  • Text HELP for assistance
  • Contact us using the details below to ask questions about your information
